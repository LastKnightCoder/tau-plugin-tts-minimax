# MiniMax Speech

Read text aloud with MiniMax Text-to-Speech. Use it for podcasts and whiteboard text-to-speech, pick from available voices, or clone a custom voice from a short audio recording.

## Getting Started

1. Install **MiniMax Speech** from the **Plugin Center**, then turn it on under the **Installed** tab on the Plugins page. It takes effect immediately without restarting.
2. Open this plugin's **Settings**, add a MiniMax account, and enter your API Key, Service URL, and model.
3. After saving, choose your default account, model, and voice in **Settings → Speech** (on mobile: **Me → Speech**). Podcasts and whiteboard narration will use it from then on.

You can add multiple accounts, each with its own saved settings. Once configured, you can preview a short sample to verify the account works; previewing does not change your selected default voice.

## Voice Cloning

Upload a clear recording to clone a new voice, or clone multiple voices at once. Recordings are sent directly to MiniMax, with no separate upload provider required.

## FAQ

**Will I still be charged after canceling?** Possibly. Canceling only stops waiting for the result; speech synthesis or voice cloning already completed by MiniMax may still be billed.

## Notes

- Speech synthesis, voice cloning, and retries all consume your MiniMax quota.
- Keep your API Key private—never share it or include it in screenshots or issue reports.
